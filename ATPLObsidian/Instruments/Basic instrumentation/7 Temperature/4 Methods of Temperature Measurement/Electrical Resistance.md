Some electrically conductive materials change resistance with temperature. The change in resistance can be measured to infer. The temperature of the material.

REsistivity changes are not usually uniform over a wide range of temperature so resistance based sensors are mostly usefull for measuring relatively narrow temperature ranges.

The big advantage of this type of measurementis that the output is a measurable eletrical property.This can be amplified and used to drive electric or electronic temperature displays. A typical application for this type of devide is air temperature measuremment
