In aviation,simple alcohol or mercury type thermometers are never used to measure temperature because they are impractical,fragileand very difficult to read.The mostbasic type oftemperature sensor is the bi-metallic strip

Different metals expand and contract by different amounts when heatedand cooler. In other words, they have different coefficients of therman expansion (CTE)

If the thermal coefficient is a carefully measured,then the changing size of a metal strip could,theoretically be used to infer this temperature.
The problem is that the change in size is quite small,we needto amplify this effect. This is achievedusing a bimetallic strip comprising two metalswith very different CTE. One of the metals has a hight CTE,the other has very low CTE

The two metals are tightly fused together. When heated, their different rates of exansion cause the bimetallic strip to bend.Even a small change in temperature can cause a significant bend in the strip. 

In the simple OAT gauges used in light aircraf,the bimetalic strip is coled and arranged so that the movement of the coilturns a pointer on a temperature scale.
