
A thermocouple works on the principle that when two different metals are in physycal contact a small voltage is produced.This voltage is directly proportional to the temperature difference between the two metals

A simple thermocouple is an arrangement of two juntions in series; one is held at a known rference temperature( the cold junction),the other is used as the detector (the hot junction)

In practie,the cold junction can be replaced with circuitry that provide a fized reference instead of relying on the temperature at the junction.This allows the devide to read the temperature rather than temperature difference.

Thermocouples have a broad operating range and are only really limited by the melting point of the materials involved.Thermocouples are often used to measure the gas temperatures inside a jet engine.
