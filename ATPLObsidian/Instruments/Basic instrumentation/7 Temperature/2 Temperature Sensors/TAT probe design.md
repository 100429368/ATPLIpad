TAT = total air temperature 

Is the mostcommonformofair temperature measurementsensor on all but light aircraft. IT works by measuring the temperature ofthe airflow after it has been slowed down relative to the aircraft.

Slowing the air to an almostcompletestop causes its temperature to rise. This is known as a RAM RISE.The amount of ramrise produced by a particular design of probe is known. Substracting ram rise from TAT gives the SAT

TAT probes aremounted on the fuselage where position error is small. To avoid radiated heat the temperature sensing element is mounted inside the body of the probe,out of direct sunlight.

The outer body of the probe comprises a scoop,into which the air enters,and a strut that positions the scoopoutside the boundary layer.The sensor is mounted in the middle of the strut.

TAT = SAT + RAM RISE 
Ram rise is the adiabatic compression(no heat transfering during the process)
Adiabática = conversión de cinética a calorífica


When the aircraft is moving, air enters the scoop. A proportion of the air exits directly through the rear of the scoop but someof it makes a 90º turn and travels along the inside strut. In doingso it is slowed down considerably.

It is important ton maintain a small amount of flow to allowthe sensor to continuously samplenew air. An exitopening in the rear of the struct exhausts the sampled air to atmosphere via a second 90ºturn

Electrical heaters prevent ice forming directly on the outervody. The probe is designed to minimise errors caused by electrical heating:
* Only the body of the probe is heated,not the sensor
* The sensor is separated from the body by air gap. Since the air is continually being replaced as the aircraft moves it does not have time to absorb heat from the body to the probe.
* The sensor is surounded by a heat shield so that it is surrounded by air in the midde of the stream rather than from the edge


The effectiveness of this design deppends upon the aircrft being in motion. When the aircraft is stationary the effects of the sun and the heating element will cause the indicated temperature to be grossly in error.
