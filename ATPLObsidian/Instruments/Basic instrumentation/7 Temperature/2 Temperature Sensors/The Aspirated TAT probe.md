The aspirated TAT probe design overcomes the problem of sensing temperature when stationary.
Pressusied air (from an engine or APU) is delivered to the probe and ejected fro the rear of the strut. This drawns air through the rest of the probe, courtesy of the jet pump effect.
Because the pressurised air is injected close to the strut exit,it never comes in contact with the sensing element.
![[Pasted image 20261113191416.png]]
The TAT probe greatly reduces the velocity of the air but it does not bring it to a completestop. Consequently the measured value is usually less than the theoretical value for TAT.

However,ADCs simply make an adjustment based on the demonstrated performance of the TAT probes fitted,and display the corrected tat. 


THEREFORE
SAT + ramrise = TAT.
