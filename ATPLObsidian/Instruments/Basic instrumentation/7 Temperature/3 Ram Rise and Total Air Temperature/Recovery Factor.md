As the air in the temperature probe is not brought to a complete halt,it willnot measure 100% of the theoretical ram rise.
As a result,the indicatedtemperature is usuallyslightly less than the TAT.

The indicated value is known as measured temperature or Ram Air Temperature (RAT).

The proportion of theoretical ram rise measured by the probe is called  recovery factor, symbollized by $K_r$, and  it will be a number less than 1.

* Measured Ram Rise = Ram Rise x $K_r$

$$SAT=\frac{TAT}{ 1+(0.2\cdot M^2 \cdot K_r)}$$
