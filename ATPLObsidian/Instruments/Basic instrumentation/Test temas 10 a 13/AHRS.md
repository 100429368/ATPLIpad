Attitude and heading refference system
It has 3 gyros - it measures in 3 axes with electronic screens

it provides horizontal and vertical components of the earth's magnetic field

AHRS includes:
1. Accelerometers
2. gyrometers
3. magnetometers

It uses MEMS gyros

It supplies:
1. Pitch attitude and rate
2. roll attitude and rate
3. yaw attitude and rate

It uses piezo-electric gyroscopes
