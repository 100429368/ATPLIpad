They are susceptible to 
* pitch errors
* roll errors
Example: after a 90º turn constant pitch and bank, it will show pitch error with too much nose up and too little bank

Acceleration -> apparent climb turn to the right (The erecting system of the instrument displaces the spin axis from vertical)
Deceleration -> apparent descending turn to the left
Turn 90º -> apparent climb, under indication of bank
turn 180º -> apparent climb, corect bank indication
turn 270º -> apparent climb, over indication of bank
turn 360º -> both pitch and roll indicated correctly.

Errors are caused by:
* Earth's rotation (latitude error)
* Aircrafts motion
* Mechanical defects of the gyro
* The gimbal mount of the gyro rings
