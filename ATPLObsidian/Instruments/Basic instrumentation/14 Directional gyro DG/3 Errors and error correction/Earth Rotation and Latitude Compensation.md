because they are horizontally tied direction gyro,DI are subject to Er drift (and it is proportional to the sine of latitude)

To improve accuracy, they introduce areal drift in the opposite direction fromthe ER drift. 

Real drift is achieved by intentionally unbalancing the inner gimbal. This is done by attaching aweight to each side of the gimbal.. One is fixed,the other,comprising a nutmounted on a thread,it is moveable. This is the LATITUDE NUT (LN)

More sophisticated DIs have a latitude nut adjuster in the cockpit. This allows the crew to adjust the amount ofinduced drift depending on the aircrafts latitude.
