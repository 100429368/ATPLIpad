
On modern CAT aircraft with ADCs, mach is not indicated by a machmeter. On older generation aircraft is usually shown on a combined Mach Airspeed Indicator (MASI) driven by the ADC.

Barber bar shows:
$V_{MO}$ Maximu operating speed
$M_{MO}$ Maximum operating speed


On 'glass cockpit' displays, mach is shown digitally,typically at the botomof the speed tape. The$V_{MO}/M_{MO}$ pointer is represented by a red hatched area at the top of the speedtape known as 'the bricks'.
