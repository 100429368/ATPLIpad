If the pitot line becomes blocked in a descent at constant speed,the pressure in the airspeed capsule remains constant but the static pressure in the case inreases. The airspeed capsule is excessively compresed and the machmeter undereads.

Conversely a blockage of the pitot in the climb causes the airspeed capsule to expand excessively. The machmeter overreads.

If you continue to mantain a constant indicated mach number in the climb after the pitot has become blocked your airspeed may reduce to the point of aerodynamic stall. In the descent,holding the same  target mach number could result in an overspeed.
