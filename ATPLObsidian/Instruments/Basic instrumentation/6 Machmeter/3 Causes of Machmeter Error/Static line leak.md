A leak in the static line affects the static pressure in the case.

In a presurised aeroplane,the higher cabin pressure enters the static line,causing an increase in the pressure in the case. This compresses both the altitude and airspeed capsules. The machmeter underreads.

In an unpressurised aeroplane the cabin pressure is maginally lower due to aerodynamicsuction. The machmeter slightly overeads.
