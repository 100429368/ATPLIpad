The conventional machmeter was an instrument which calclated and displayed the aircraft's match number using a mecanical computing system. Conventional machmeter instrumentsof this type are no longer used
Nowadays the ADC mathematically calculates the Mach number and displays it on the pilots' PFDs

The conventional instrument comprises an airspeed capsule that expands with dynamic pressure, a partially evacuated aneroid altitude capsule and a case connected to static pressure.

![[Pasted image 20261113170235.png]]

The airsped capsule operates through a long lever called the ratio arm.
The ratio arm operates on the ranging arm to move the needle pointer. The movement of the ratio armis moderated by the expansion and contraction of the altitude capsule.

If either the capsule expands, the indicated mach number increase (this can happen when speed or altitude increases).
