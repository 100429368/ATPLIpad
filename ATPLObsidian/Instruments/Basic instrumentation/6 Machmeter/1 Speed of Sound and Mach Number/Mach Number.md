Mach number(N) is a ratio of the TAS to the LS and therefore has no units.It is determined by:

$$Mach\space Number=\frac{TAS}{LSS}$$

Mach 1.0 is the LSS. This is written as M1.0.

Mach number is very important to high performance CAT aircraft because it indicates the degree of compressibility of the air. Compressibility affects light and drag and determinates the likelihood of shockwaves forming around the wings.


It can also be written as

$$Mach\space Number=\frac{TAS}{LSS} = \frac{P_d \cdot \rho}{P_d \cdot \rho} = \frac{P_d}{P_d}$$


Tener en cuenta que manteniendo una IA constante, si bajamos,la Pd baja,por tanto, sube el mach number.
