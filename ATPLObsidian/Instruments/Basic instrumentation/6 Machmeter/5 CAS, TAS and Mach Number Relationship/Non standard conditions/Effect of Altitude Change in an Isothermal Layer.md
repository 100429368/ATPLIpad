
Above the tropopause,and sometimesin the troposphere,the atmosphere is isothermal (no change of temperature with height)

In the stratosphere,and isothermal layers in the troposphere, the relatioship between CAS and TAS remains the same,but thee TAS/Mach relationship changes.

LSS depends on temperaure so if there is no temperature change,LSS will not change with height. 

A climb or descent at constant Mach results in constant TAS
