Because EAS is CAS corrected for compressibility error, EAS is always the same as,or lower than CAS.

In a descent from high altitude at constant M,the EAS increases slightly more rapidly than the CAS. A usefulmemory aid is: extra chicken tikka masalaxddd

If the air temperature changes when flying at the same flight level:
* At a constant M,the CASremains the same. By the same reasoning flying at the same CAS,the M does not change
* TAS increases when flying into warmer air and reduces when flying into colder air


**Temperature up, TAS up; temperature down, TAS down**
