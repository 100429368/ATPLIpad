E type compasses are simple and reliable, but they are not easy to interpret. Because of where they need to be mounted, you have to look at the rear of the compass card. 
The lubber line represents the aircraft tail. The compass card has to be marked reverse so it reads correctly.
The result is that the compass cardappears to turn the 'wrong' way when you alter the heading. This takes quite a bit getting used to it.
