For a balanced turn,bank is applied together with a small amount of rudder into the turn.
There are some occasions when we deliberately fly out of balance:
* Duing crosswind take off,or landing, using the wig down technique
* 
* Slide slipping to lose height on approach
* During some aerobatic manoeuvres
* During assymetricflight using a very slight out of balance condition to reduce overall dragfollowing an engine failure
