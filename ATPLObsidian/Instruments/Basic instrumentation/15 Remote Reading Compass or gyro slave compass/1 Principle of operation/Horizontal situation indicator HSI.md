Is the electromechanical predecessor of the moder EFIS navigation display.

It is laced beneath the attitude indicator in the place of the conventional DI on a standard six-instrument arrangement.

The HSI is a remote reading compass,providing heading information by means of a compass card,similar to a direction indicator. However the magnetic north reference for the heading informtion is continuously obtained frothe flux valve.
![[Pasted image 20261125181911.png]]

HSI combines compass card(providing heading information) with a course deviation indicator (CDI) which permits the pilot to carry out lateral navigation to and from VHF omnidirecional radio(VOR) navigation beaconds and lateral plus vertical navigation along instrument landing system (ILS).
