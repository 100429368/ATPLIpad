Vertical Speed Indicator also known as rate of climb indicator

They can have
Blocked static port
Instrument error
Time lag
Position error

It uses TWO STATIC PRESSURE VALUES

When static is blocked, the needle will turn to 0.
When total is blocked, the needle continues to indicate normally 

When multi crew, if you have more than 1 port, they are connected then if a side is affected, both sides are affected, they will be unaccurated, But not 0

the response of the detector can be improved by adding correction based on accelerometer sensor

Since the display is logarithmic, a small change of rate is displayed more accurately than a large rate of change
