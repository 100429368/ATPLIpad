Light twin-engine aeroplanes may have two additional coloured lines. 
These are:
* $V_{MCA}$ . A red line shows Minimum control speed in the air. This is the minimum speed at which directional control can be maintained following an engine failure with the other engine at fullpower. With one engine failed,if speed reduces below $V_{MCA}$ during the flare you are committed to landing. If you attempt to go around you will lose control of the aeroplane.
* $V_{YSE}$. A blue line. This shows the speed for best rate of climbon the engine. Alight twin has a very limited ability to climb on the engine. The blu lines gives you a quick,easily identifiable target sped. Usefull when you are under a great deal of stres.
