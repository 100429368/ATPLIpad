Two 'V' speed rateto the landing gear:
* Maximum operating speed with the landing gear operating $V_{LO}$ 
* Maximum operating speed with the landing gear extended $V_{LE}$

$V_{LE}$ is often fasterthan $V_{LO}$ because the gearand doors can withstand higher dynamic pressures once locked down thatn while in the process of extending or retracing. A gear-down ferry flight would therefore be limited to the faster $V_{LE}$
