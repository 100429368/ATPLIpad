
The angle of attack vane is mounted on the fuselage. This is pivoted at the front,wellin front of its centre of pressure. The vane is free to rotate around its pivot.

In flight the airflow forces the vane to rotate about its pivot untilit is precisely in the line with the flow. A transduced reads the vane's angle to give a direct reading of AoA.

This can be damaged by aggresive landings.
