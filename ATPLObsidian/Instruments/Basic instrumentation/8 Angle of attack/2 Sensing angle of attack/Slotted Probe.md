It has two parts. When the probe is pointing directly into the airflow each port senses the same pressure.

TThe probe is mounted on the aircraft so that balanced pressure occurs at 0º angleof attack(alpha)

At any angle of attack other than 0º,the two ports sense different pressure values. Higher  pressure in the lower port indicates a possitive angle of attack. The difference in pressure is measured to infer the angle of attack.
