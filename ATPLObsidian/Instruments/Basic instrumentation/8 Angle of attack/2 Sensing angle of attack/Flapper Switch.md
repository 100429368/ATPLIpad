The very simplest method for estimating the angle of attack is the flapper switch. 

The switch is mounted on the leading edge of the wing and is spring loaded to the off position. At a certain high angle of attack(just before the critical angle)the increased upwash at the leading edge causes the switch to flip tothe position. This closes an electrical circuit to power a stall warning horn.


This system cannot measure the angle of attack,it can only indicate when it becomes excesive.
