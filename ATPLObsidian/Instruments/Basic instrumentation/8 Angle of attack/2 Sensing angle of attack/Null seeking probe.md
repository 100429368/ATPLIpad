Senses differential pressure. However,the sensor is mounted on a rotor which is positioned by a servo motor.

Whenever a diferential pressure is sensed,the servo motor drives the probe, rotating it until the pressure sensed in both ports is equal.
The resulting probe angle is measured to determine the angle of attack.

This type of probe is usually designed as a pivoting slotted cylinder (rotor)mounted at right angles of the fuselage.
