SPS is the mean sea level pressure of 1013.25hPa in ISA conditions.

In reality the mean sea level pressure is never 1013.25 hPa at all points of the Earth at the same time. Nevertheless, it is essential to have a common datum which all aircraft use whenever they are not inclose proximity to terrain.

An altimeter whose sub-scale setting is set to sps (1013.25) will read the height above the 1013.25 pressure livil (which for the reasons given above is not usually the height above mean sea level)

Heights above 1013.25 pressure level are knowin as FLIGHT LEVELS
