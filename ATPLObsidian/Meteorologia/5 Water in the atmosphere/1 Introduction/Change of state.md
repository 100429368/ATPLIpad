Transitions from a less organized to a more organized state.
Hidden heat (calor oculto o calor latente) is the one that allows this change of state.
Calores de cambio de estado - calor LATENTE.

Transitions: 
* Heat taken in (se absorbe)
	* Melting :ice to water (liquid)
	* Evaportation: water to vapour
	* Sublimation :ice to vpour without water as intermediate.
* Heatgiven out: (se cede)
	* Condensation: vapour to water
	* Freezing
	* Sublimation or deposition: vapour directy to ice without water as intermediate.
