Given the full composition of air such as [[ATPLObsidian/Meteorologia/5 Water in the atmosphere/2 Basics/Composition of the atmosphere|Composition of the atmosphere]] is named DRY AIR , if we dont count the water.

If we compare the mass of water vapor with the volume of air (dry) we obtain the water vapor density or *absolute density* 

$$ Absolute = \frac{MasaTotal}{VolumenDryAir} [g/m^3]$$
