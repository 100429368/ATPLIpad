Is used in ATIS and VOLMET brodcasts when the visibility is good and there are no observable weather conditions. Specifically it means:
1. Visibility is 10km or more
2. No cloud of operational significance.This is often considered to mean
	1. No cloud below 5000ft agl or below the minimum sector altitude (MSA)
	2. No comulonimbus or towering cumulus clouds
3. No significant weather
