Is a voice radio broadcast comprising aerodrome weather reports. It is a broadcast on VHF or HF.
Unlike ATIS, each VOLMET station gives weather observations for several aerodromes in its locality. 

Content of VOLMET is similar to ATIS:
* Aerodrome idenetification
* Surface wind
* Visibility
* RVR, if applicable
* Weather conditions
* Cloud
* Temperature and dew point
* QNH
* Trend,if applicable.
