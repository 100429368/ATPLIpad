A runway’s surface is asserted for the type and depth of contaminant along each third of its length , starting with the touchdown end. Using this information , couplwd with pilots reported experiences of deceleratiom amd directional control, a runway condition code is created (RWYCC)
The reported RWYCC is used by operators and pilots in take off amd landing performance calculations.
