Every ATIS,D-ATIS and volmet broadcast includes the reported meteorological visibility. Reported visibility is the distance the met observer and the aerodrome can se.

Airports may also report the runway visual range(RVR). RVRis a more accurate measure of the visibility you will encounter on take-off and landing.  It is usually measured by atomated measuring equipment to calculate an instrumented RVR(IRVR)

RVRs may included in the ATIS broadcast. For arriving aircraft,the most revent RVR is reported by the approach controller. The crewuse this to decide wether they can legally being, or continue,the approach.

Minimum acceptable RVR depends on the type of approach,airport,equipment and procedures, aircraft equipment and crew qualifications.

Instrumented RVR is measured in three places on the runway::
1. Touchdown zone
2. Mid point
3. Stop end

When transmitting runway visual range the abbreviation RVR may be used without using the phonetic alphabet. Multiple RVRs are always transmitted in metres starting ith the reading for the touchdown zone.

The runway designator may be omitted if there is no possibilityof confusion. There is no requirement to read back RVRs
