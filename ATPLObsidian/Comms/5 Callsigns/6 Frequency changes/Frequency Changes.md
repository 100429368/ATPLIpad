A controller instructig an aircraft to transfer communications to another air trafic services unit will pass the identity and frequency to the unit to be contacted. You must readback the frequency. 
For IFR flights, the new ATSU will alreadybe expecting the handover.

If you obtain no response after changing to the new frequency, and after waiting for an appropiate length of time,revert back to the previous frequency.

In controled airspace,you ust receive permission before changing frequency. In uncontrolled airpsace you may request or simply advisethat you are changing to a new frequency.In this case,the new ATSU may not be expectingyour call and may be unaware of your flight details.
