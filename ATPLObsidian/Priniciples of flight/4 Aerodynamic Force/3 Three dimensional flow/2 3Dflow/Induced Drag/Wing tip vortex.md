Torbellino de punta de plano

We assume that in the wing tip appears a movement analogous to a vortex.
This secondary flux from [[Helicoidal vortexes]] is a vortex. 


In a rectangle wing, there is only wing tipvortex. But in a non rectangular wing , there are a subsequence of different vortices in the wing, but in any case the most intense one is the one in the tip. If we considere the biggest one , we can more or less cover the other ones.
