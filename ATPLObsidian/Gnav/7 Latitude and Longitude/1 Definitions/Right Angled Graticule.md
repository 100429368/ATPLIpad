The intersection of paralells of latitude and meridians of longitude create a right-angledgraticule. A graticule is a network of lines representing prallelsof latitude and meridians of longitude on a map or chart.
They are principal references for creating an accurate map or chart because each location on the chart is positioned by reference to the graticule.

A right-angled graticule is a graticule in which the intersecting lines cross each other at 90º.
