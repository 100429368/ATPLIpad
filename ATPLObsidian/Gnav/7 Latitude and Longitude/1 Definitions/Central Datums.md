* Latitude and the Equator
An equator is an imaginary line around the middleof a celestial body.It is located halfway(equidistant) betwen the North andsouth Poles, at a latitude of N/S00º. An equator divides a planet into a a Northern Hemisphere and Southern Hemisphere. The Earth is widest at its Equator

* Longitude and the Greenwich Meridian
A prime meridian is an imaginary line, running from the South Pole to the North Pole. It is the datum for defining positions and measuring distances both east and west.
Unlike an equator which has to be halfway between the poles,aprie meridian is arbitrary, meaning it could be chosen to be anywwhere.
