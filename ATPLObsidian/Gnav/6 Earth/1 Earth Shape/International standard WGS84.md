The most universally accepted version for the Earth reference ellipsoid,required for geographical coordinates in aviation and used for productions of accurate navigational charts and data.

A number of different ellipsoids areused to desgribe the shape of Earth for mapping but at WGS84 is the reference ellipsoid required for geographical coordinates

Reference datum for the NAVSTAR/GPS (usa) and GALILEO (europe) GNSS

Main parameters :
* WGS84 datum surface is an oblateshperoid (ellipsoid)
* The origin (centre) of WGS84 is located at the centre of the Earth with an error ofles than 2cm
* The accuracy of the WGS84 ellipsoid is approximately 10cm
* The major (equatorial) radius(semi-major axis) = 6378km
* The polar (semi-minor) axis = 6357km
