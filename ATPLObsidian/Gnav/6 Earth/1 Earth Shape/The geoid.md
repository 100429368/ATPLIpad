Is a term used to describe the irregular shape of the Earth,based on the surface of the oceans influenced only by gravity and centrifugal force(but notsubjectto wind and tide).

Is the equipotential surface that would coincide with the mean ocean surface of Earth, if all oceans and the overlying atmosphere were in a state of equilibrium,considered to be at rest relative to the rotating Earth, and extended through the land masses. 

It has a highly irregular surface, the shape of which is the result of an uneven distribution of mass,within and on the surface of the Earth. The geoid does not correspond exactly to the actual surface of the Earth but it is often described as the true physical shape of the Earth.

While the geoid can be said to be almost the actual shape of the Earth, reference ellipsoid is a mathematical model which approximates the geoid but is highly regular in shape. Reference ellipsoids are very important concept for satellite geodesy: the science of measuring the Earth
