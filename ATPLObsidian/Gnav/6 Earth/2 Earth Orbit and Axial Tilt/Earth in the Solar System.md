* Ecliptic
The ecliptit is the path in the Earth's sky that the Sun appearsto follow in tan easterly direction over the duration of the year, with the sun being overhead different positions on the surface of the Earth throughout the year. The plane of the solar ecliptic is coplanar with Earth's orbit around the Sun.

The Earth rotates towards the east on its vertical axis,anti-clockwise when viewed from above to northern hemisphere. 

when viewed from the same position,the Earth also follows the orbital parth around the Sun along the plane of the ecliptic,in the same 'easterly'direction that it rotates around its own vertical axis.
* Axial Tilt
The Earth's axis of rotation is tilted atan angle approximately 66.5º relative to the ecliptic. The Earth's equatorial plane is at an angle of approximately 23.5ºto the plane of the ecliptic

The Earth's orbit around the Sun,along the ecliptic,is slightly elliptical.

The elliptical orbit,and the axial tilt,account for our changing seasons and also for the change in sunrise,sunsted and twilight times throughout theyear, giving us changing lengths of days.
