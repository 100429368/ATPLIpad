As each 24h period passes,the mean and apparent solar days move gradually in out of synchronisation. Over the course of a year, apparent time lags or leads mean timeby about +16.5 o-14minutes

In simple terms
* The position of the midday Apparent Sun is when directly overhead the observer's local meridian,irrespective of the time.
* the position of the Midday Mean Sun varies, but always ocurs at 12:00 noon,irrespective of its position (which is not significantly different frombeing overhead the observer's local meridian)
